Private events & catering

At Dolce Vita Craft & Kitchen we aim to work with you to create the perfect menu for your event, and we listen closely to your requirements and requests. We are proud of our flexibility when it comes to making everything exactly as you want it, however lavish or simple it may be. Our food is based on classical dishes with a modern influence, and provides value to our clients through quality food, presentation and service. Whatever the event, whether it's your birthday or your engagement party, it's a big deal — and you want to make sure the catering is perfect.

Here at Dolce Vita we have years of experience in private party catering, and our kitchen team is primed and ready to get creative and prepare an innovative, bespoke menu to suit your celebration and satisfy your vision. For birthdays, celebrations or work events, Dolce Vita opens its doors outside our usual hours too. Get in touch to talk it through.
